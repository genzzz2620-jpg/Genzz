# Genzz AI v1.0.0 — Release Notes

Genzz AI helps users practice interviews with AI-generated preparation tools. Availability of provider-backed features depends on deployment configuration. AI-generated content is practice assistance; it is not guaranteed to match an employer's interview questions, hiring criteria, or outcomes.

## Included capabilities

- AI interview practice sessions and configurable answer generation.
- Windows desktop interview workspace with user-controlled microphone permission and speech-to-text where supported by the runtime.
- Resume upload/processing and Resume Maker workflows.
- Question Bank, interview history, and session feedback.
- Preparation Planner and AI Interview Simulator.
- Company and job preparation based on supplied information.
- Notifications, usage analytics, credit wallet, and premium subscription flows when configured.
- Administrative dashboards and audit capabilities for authorized operators.

## Availability notes

OpenAI, Gemini, Stripe checkout, email delivery, durable production storage, and monitoring require operator configuration. The application does not include password-reset/email-verification flows or automatic account deletion. Do not treat this document as a claim that production deployment or release certification has passed; see `PHASE_32_PRODUCTION_LAUNCH_REPORT.md`.
